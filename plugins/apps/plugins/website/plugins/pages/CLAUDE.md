# pages

The site's inner pages — one plugin each: the vision for applications
(`/website/apps`), the technical foundations (`/website/foundations`) and the
story (`/website/story`).

Each owns its `/website/<segment>` pane, and its own `Section` render slot, so
the content can be written later as contributions without touching the page
plugin. Apps and Story also put a link in the shared site header; Foundations is
reached from its layer card on the homepage.

All three are placeholders today: the page's heading (the shell's
`WebsiteHero`, the same way every page opens) and the shell's `WebsiteSoon` note
under it. When a page's sections are written, remove its `WebsiteSoon`.
