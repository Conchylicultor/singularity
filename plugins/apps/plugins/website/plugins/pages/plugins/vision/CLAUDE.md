# vision

The vision page at `/website/vision` — the vision for applications. (The
apps themselves are listed by its sibling, the gallery at `/website/apps`.)

A placeholder today: the page's heading ("A vision for applications" and why
now), then the site's "more details soon" note. It owns the
`WebsiteVision.Section` slot the page will be written into, so writing it means
adding a plugin, never editing this one — and removing the `WebsiteSoon` note
from the pane.

The nav link is contributed into the shell's shared `WebsiteHeader`, so the
shell never names this page. The homepage's third layer card (the vision) and
the apps gallery's read-next card open it.
