# hero

The homepage's headline band — the site's one sentence, and the paragraph under
it that sets up the two questions.

This is the only centred band on the site, and the only one that paints anything
of its own: an ambient accent wash behind it, and the same accent gradient
painted through the emphasised half of the headline. Both are in
`hero-section.css`, and both read the active theme's tokens rather than pinning
colours — so a preset swap re-tints the hero with everything else. Never
hardcode a colour here.

The register is still an essay's, not a pricing page's: the headline makes one
claim and the paragraph says why it is a bet. Then it hands over to
[`fork`](../fork/CLAUDE.md), which asks the questions.
