# contact

Everything about reaching the person behind equin: the homepage's closing band,
and the "Get in touch" call to action in the shared site header.

One plugin rather than two because there is one address, and it is declared here
(`web/internal/contact.ts`). A second plugin owning the header CTA would be a
second copy of the address, and the two would drift.

The two cards lead to the same inbox. The split is two READERS — someone who wants
to back this, and someone who is stuck on the same problem — not two inboxes;
naming which one you are is what makes the email easy to start. Do not add a form:
there is no backend behind this site, and a form that goes nowhere is worse than
an address.
