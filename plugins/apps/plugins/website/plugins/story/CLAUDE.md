# story

The story page — where equin came from, and the context behind the two questions.

Same shape as the two question pages: the page states its opening, then renders
whatever is contributed to `WebsiteStory.Section`. Nothing is contributed yet, and
that is deliberate — an unwritten page reads as unwritten rather than as a page
with a placeholder on it. Write the story as a new sub-plugin under here, not as
an edit to this one.

The homepage links here through
[`landing/story-link`](../landing/plugins/story-link/CLAUDE.md); keep the two
sets of words the same, so following the link confirms rather than re-introduces.
