# chord

A chord ear trainer: it plays a short loop of a real song and asks you to name
its chords, only ever choosing loops made of chords you have already unlocked.
The track page is `block-49ba706c-affe-417b-a9a1-b6873e8c7ea8` ("Chord trainer
app"); the design is `research/2026-09-16-apps-chord-trainer-song-index*.md`
(v1–v4).

What exists today:

- `shell/core` — the app's identity (`chordApp`, id `chord`). No pane or rail
  entry yet.
- `data-dirs` — the app's one data dir, `apps/chord/`. The song index keeps its
  snapshot in the `song-index` area.
- `song-index` — the index of TheoryTab sections and their 4-bar loop windows,
  loaded on first use, and the endpoints that pick loops. See its CLAUDE.md.
