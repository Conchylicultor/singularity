# hover-popover

`<HoverPopover trigger content label …/>` — a popover revealed by **hover**,
for a trigger whose click already does something else ("click acts, hover
previews": the app launcher's mark navigates on click and shows the app grid on
hover). For a click-to-open popover use `overlay/popover`'s `InlinePopover`.

- **Hover** opens after `openDelay` (120 ms) and closes `closeDelay` (200 ms)
  after the pointer has left BOTH the trigger and the portaled panel — both
  feed one `createHoverIntent` (core), so crossing the gap never closes it.
- **Keyboard** — ArrowDown on the trigger opens it and focuses the panel's first
  control; Esc closes and returns focus to the trigger. Focus leaving the panel
  or an outside press closes it.
- **Touch** has no hover: the first tap on a closed trigger opens (the click is
  stopped in the capture phase, so the trigger's own `onClick` never sees it);
  the next tap clicks.
- The trigger's click is never a toggle: base-ui's `trigger-press` is ignored.
  A click acts, and closes the panel.

`createHoverIntent` (core) is the framework-free timing (open intent, grace
close, re-entry always cancels the pending close). `floating-action`'s
`useDisclosureIntent` uses it too — reuse it rather than hand-rolling timers.
