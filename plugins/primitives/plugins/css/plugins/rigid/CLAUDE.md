# rigid

The **rigid flex child** — a leaf that keeps its size while its siblings absorb
the pressure. `shrink-0`, named once. 38 call sites wrote it as their only banned
token because nothing owned it.

## One of four space-sharing roles

A flex child answers **does it take slack?** and **does it give below its own
content?** Rigid is no to both.

| role | classes | axis? | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **`rigidClass()`** | `shrink-0` | **no** | won't give at all |
| [`yieldClass(axis)`](../yield/CLAUDE.md) | `min-w-0` \| `min-h-0` | yes | gives only |
| [`growClass()`](../grow/CLAUDE.md) | `flex-1` | no | takes only |
| [`fillClasses(axis)`](../fill/CLAUDE.md) | `flex-1 min-w-0` | yes | both (= grow + yield) |

**A sibling plugin, not a prop on `fill`.** The governing precedent is
[`clip`](../clip/CLAUDE.md) / [`scroll`](../scroll/CLAUDE.md): two halves of one
mechanic, kept orthogonal in two plugins. `Stack`/`Inset` cohabit `spacing` only
because they share the `SpaceStep` ramp; `fillClasses` and `rigidClass` share no
data at all.

## No axis prop — and that asymmetry is deliberate

Only the `min-*-0` half of the family takes an axis, because `min-width:0` and
`min-height:0` are two **different CSS properties** and only one is right for a
given container. `flex-shrink` (here) and `flex-grow` (`growClass()`) are single
properties that already apply along whichever axis the container declared as its
main axis. An axis argument here could only be ignored — or, worse, believed.
Don't add one.

## Take the class, keep the element

`rigidClass()` is the **default answer**. 34 of the 38 sites already sit on an
element the author owns and styles (`w-40 shrink-0 truncate font-mono …`);
wrapping those in a `<Rigid>` would add a box whose only job is to hold one
class.

`<Rigid>` is for the two remaining shapes:

- the rigid **spacer** with no content of its own — `<Rigid className="w-16" />`
  (the Gantt column spacers);
- a rigid wrapper around children you cannot otherwise reach.

## Whose job rigidity is

It belongs to a leaf that **travels**: "must not shrink inside whatever flex row
hosts me" is knowledge the leaf has and its container does not. A named-slot
container owns rigidity for its **own** slots instead — `<Column>` already makes
its header/footer rigid — and that split stays. A per-child role prop on
`Stack`/`Row`/`Line` was rejected: annotating children needs `cloneElement` or
slots-as-props, which is what `<Frame>` did before it was deleted.
