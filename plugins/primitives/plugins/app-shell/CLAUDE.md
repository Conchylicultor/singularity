# app-shell

## Sidebar nav entries: `opens` (data) xor `onClick`

A nav entry (`AppShellSidebarNav`) says what a click does in one of two arms:

- **`opens: opensPane(pane, params)`** — a pane, as data. The shell opens it as
  the route root AND marks the row active (`SidebarMenuButton isActive`) while
  the route's root pane is that pane, so the current section is highlighted
  with nothing to keep in sync. Prefer it whenever the entry opens a pane.
- **`onClick`** — anything else. Never highlighted: a callback cannot say where
  it goes.

The row's geometry (height, padding, icon size, icon gap, label weight) and the
panel width are the `ui/tokens/sidebar-metrics` theme tokens; the icon wears
`--sidebar-icon` at rest and the row's text colour on hover / when active.

## The brand: one `AppShell.Brand`, never a `header` prop

`AppShellLayout` takes no header. The top-left brand is the single
`AppShell.Brand` contribution (`{ component: ComponentType<{ form }> }`,
today `apps-core/app-launcher`), so every app shows the same one by
construction and the shell stays ignorant of what it is. The shell decides
where it goes:

- **with a sidebar** — `form="header"` heads the sidebar (the framing's
  `header`);
- **without a sidebar** — `form="icon"` is the leading edge of the top chrome:
  `SurfaceChromeContext.leadingControl` (the first pane header, where the
  sidebar toggle would sit), or the chrome toolbar's first item when one
  renders.

No contribution → nothing is drawn and no empty header is reserved. An app
whose chrome bar is its own rather than `AppShellLayout`'s renders
`<AppShellBrand form="icon"/>` at that bar's leading edge.

Every mounted `AppShellBrand` is recorded against its surface (the tab id from
`surface-id`); `useBrandDrawnOn(surfaceId)` answers whether a surface shows the
brand, so global chrome that stands in for it (the solo-mode floating launcher)
reads what is on screen rather than a per-app flag.
