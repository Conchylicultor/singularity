# hook-message

`hook_system_message` is a hook printing something informational — "run
/ultrareview before you push", "3 free reviews left". Nothing failed and
nothing was blocked.

That is why this is a separate plugin from `hook-error` rather than another
subtype inside it. `hook-error` renders a destructive, expanded-by-default card
because its rows are outcomes the reader must not scroll past; a tip rendered in
that chrome would teach the reader to ignore the colour. The two hook families
are told apart by their chrome, so they cannot be told apart by their contents.

There is also nothing to expand: the payload is one sentence plus the hook's
identity. So it is an `EventLine`, with the hook name in the label using the
same `label · hook` grammar as the `hook-success` and `hook-error` cards, and
the message truncated on the line beside it. A card whose body only restates
its own label would be noise.
