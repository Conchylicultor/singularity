# deferred-tools

The harness tells the agent which tools are deferred — present by name, but with
no schema loaded until something asks for one — in two spellings:

- `deferred_tools_record`, the full roster, and
- `deferred_tools_delta`, the tools that just appeared or vanished.

**One plugin owns both**, because they are one subject. Split across two
plugins, a reader scrolling a transcript would meet two rows that look unrelated
— a card called something else, a different note grammar, a different body
shape — and have to work out for themselves that both answer "which deferred
tools exist right now". So the two share the label `Deferred tools`, and only
the note distinguishes them: a count (`· 16 tools`) for the roster, `(+2 −1)`
for the change. Owning both here also means a future change to the family — a
new spelling, a different body — lands in one place instead of drifting apart.

The roster's body lists tool **names only**. Every entry carries a
`description` that is the tool's whole schema prose, hundreds of lines apiece;
sixteen of those pasted into the conversation would bury it for a fact almost
nobody is looking for. The row's raw-JSON action is the place for the full
entries, which is exactly what it is for.

(The plugin was called `deferred-tools-delta` while the delta was the only
spelling it rendered.)
