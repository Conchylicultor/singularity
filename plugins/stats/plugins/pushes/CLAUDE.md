# pushes

Push contention stats, as SQL over the op-store's `op_log_ops` table
(`_opLogOps` from `op-log/plugins/op-store/server` — the DB fold of
`op-log.jsonl`). Every handler reads through `completedPushes(bucket)`
(`server/internal/read-pushes.ts`), so they cannot disagree on what counts.

## What counts

- `kind = 'push'`, closed (`closed_by IS NOT NULL`) with an outcome.
- **Not in flight.** An unfinished push has no final outcome, its wait is still
  growing and it has no steps yet — counting it would make a chart depend on
  when it was loaded.
- **Not interrupted.** Hard-killed and closed by the reconciler (or a worktree's
  ingest-gap close): a synthetic zero, not a measurement.

History is the op store's: 30 days (its retention), from the ingester's first
seed onward — the frozen pre-op-log legacy files are not in it.

Buckets key on `requested_at` in UTC (`YYYY-MM-DD`; the ISO week's Monday;
`YYYY-MM`). The wait is `closed_wait_ms` = `sum(waits.durationMs)` — every wait
the push blocked on, including the nested host-grant wait inside its `checks`
step.

Three aggregate chart endpoints, each `?bucket=day|week|month` (default `day`):

- **Wait time** — avg/max wait (seconds) over the contested pushes, plus
  `contested` / `total` counts.
- **Throughput** — success vs failed pushes per bucket (every non-success
  terminal is failed).
- **Step breakdown** — avg seconds per push in each step group (fetch, rebase,
  checks, push, other); steps come from `jsonb_array_elements(steps)`, grouped
  by the `STEP_GROUPS` name map in TS.
