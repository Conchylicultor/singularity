Worth noting: I only made the positive checks retry. Checks expecting “there should be zero of these” still look once. If you retry those, you'd accept the first moment something hasn't appeared yet and call it a pass — which would quietly stop catching real bugs.

What's still broken

Two things I did not fix, both the same underlying problem.

Follow-up filed

Bulleted and numbered lists still aren't lists. A screen reader should say “list, 5 items” and count you through them. Right now it just reads five unrelated lines — the bullet character itself is decorative and deliberately unspoken.

Quotes and callouts have no identity either. The box around a quotation is visible to you but invisible to a screen reader; it can't tell you where the quote starts or ends.

Both need something the page can't currently provide: a container. Marking something a “list item” requires a surrounding “list” element, and a quote needs an element that actually wraps the quoted passage. But the editor lays every block out as a flat sequence of siblings on purpose — that's precisely what keeps your cursor alive when you indent or move a block. Adding a wrapper would reintroduce the destroy-and-rebuild problem.

There's a known technique for this, but it carries real risk and it belongs to a different part of the system, so I filed both as follow-up tasks rather than half-doing them.

One more thing I found but left alone, because it's a different bug from the one you asked about: the checkbox on a to-do block has no name at all. A screen reader announces “checkbox, not checked” and nothing else — it can't tell you which checkbox. That's arguably worse than the heading gap I just fixed, so it's your call whether to bundle it in or file it separately.
